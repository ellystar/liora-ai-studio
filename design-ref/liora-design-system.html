01 — FOUNDATION

The brand, before the pixels.

Every token below descends from one idea. The system exists to keep that idea intact while production scales — to preserve and author at the same time.

Signature concept

AI can generate visuals.
Only aesthetic intelligence can preserve a brand.

The category gets Liora into the room: an AI-native creative systems lab. Aesthetic intelligence — the system of judgment that decides how a brand looks, sounds and feels — is why it stays.

Hero verbs

Two words carry the whole company. Every component, every microcopy decision serves them.

preserve author
The enemy

The system is built against one thing — sameness: the flattening of everything distinctive into the generic. Speed is not the enemy. Noise is.

02 — FOUNDATION

Color is a register, not a palette.

Warm-neutral dominant: cream grounds, a warm near-black for the dark turns. The page never shouts — the drama is contrast, not hue. Accent is a separate, deliberate decision, and each accent has exactly one job.

Italic discipline

Italic is never decorative. It is reserved for the words that carry the brand's meaning — preserve, author, soul — and for the slogan. Used everywhere, it would mean nothing; used rarely, it marks the soul of a sentence.

Whitespace is the institution.

The calm of a research institution comes from air. A single 4px-based scale governs every gap; sections breathe at 128–160; the page is generous before it is dense.

05 — FOUNDATION

The torn seam is the one gesture.

Boldness is spent in a single place. The torn seam — the cut between an old master and its AI continuation — is Liora's signature: craft meeting machine, preserve meeting author. Everything around it stays quiet.

Surfaces
Flat elevationCards sit on hairlines, never shadows. Border Stone, radius 2px.
Halftone fieldBreathes in margins only — corner-masked, low opacity. Never a full field.
Signature — torn seam
Old master · craft AI · continuation

Construction: an irregular near-vertical tear, 1–4° off-axis, with a torn-paper edge. In production it's a real torn-paper alpha mask over two images — here it's rendered in CSS to communicate the motif.

Microcopy is design material. Buttons name what happens; the system never leads with automation, generate, prompt or agency — even though it does all of those internally.

Imagery — art direction

Warm classical × contemporary: old-master painting torn into an AI/model continuation. Real artworks (Botticelli, Vermeer, etc.) are not reproduced here for licensing reasons — production uses commissioned or licensed composites. Treatment: warm grade, soft depth, generous negative space, the torn seam as the recurring device. Never cold, never stocky, never a full halftone field.

08 — SYSTEM LOGIC

Slow, deliberate, never playful.

Sophisticated is not the same as complex. Motion is editorial and human: a measured page-load, a quiet scroll reveal, micro-hovers. Nothing bounces; nothing announces itself.

09 — IDENTITY

A wordmark, quietly set.

For launch: no symbol. Like a research institution, a publisher, a house — the name, in the display face, with generous tracking. The restraint is the statement.
